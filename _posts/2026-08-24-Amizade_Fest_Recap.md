Last 4th of July 2026, me and my band had the pleasure to play at what was probably one of the most beautiful live music (and not only) events that we've experienced. It started out as an invite from a friend of ours, telling us that they were working on a brand new festival called amizade fest and that they wanted us to play a set as Prima Augusta.

I reluctantly said yes as this invite came at a time that I was really struggling with my mental health. I couldn't find happiness most days and was questioning my friendships, my relationship, my job, my future and my abilities in general. I felt sick and weak, felt completely broken down, and it was without a doubt the worst time I can remember. 

I couldn't tell you for sure what got me up, but I can tell that the invite for amizade fest must have helped. Prima Augusta hadn't played live in almost two years, so there was a feeling of urgency to start playing again. First rehearsals were pretty rough, but we got up to snatch pretty quickly. I promptly started working on a Ableton live project that we could use live for drum tracks.

> I hope to write about technical details from this live set and some of the Ableton hacks that I made using max in order to automate certain tasks that I use on stage :)
> This was heavily inspired by Ed's (best youth) live set workshop for Porto's Ableton user group 

Someone then reached out to us asking if we'd like to perform live on a band contest also in santa maria da feira. We of course said yes, thinking that even if we didn't win something, at least it would be an opportunity for practising and learning how we would set up our live sound. This gave us valuable experience, especially while gauging the reactions from sound engineers to our tech rider :) and we even got a cash prize

### Amizade fest

So how was the festival? I have to give congratulations to everyone that was involved in making it happen. From the get go, the organisation made every detail of the festival transparent to the artists, and in the end all profits were equally divided between all performing live acts. For the first edition it was, beyond a doubt, a success. Tickets were sold out by the day before, and the turn out was way more than what I had anticipated.

The soundcheck went well, there were several delays due to 7 bands sharing the same stage. Despite that, even with he in-ear monitors our setup was pretty smooth and in the end the sound at the front of house was great (at least from the recordings, my monitoring mix was really bad). I think that our biggest issue were the nerves. This new set was 30 minutes of just original songs which we are still working on, so even what we had rehearsed was still very fresh.

Our soft going music style didn't really fit in with the rest, but still we felt right at home and are proud to say that made friends with every band there. Besides, it was nice to see friends I hadn't seen in years come together to see us play and party.

![Pomadinha](/img/2026-08-24-Amizade_Fest_Recap/pomadinha.png)

> The night ended on such a high note, with Marques 2069 closing with an absolutely banger DJ set. I hope they don't remember my drunken requests for New Order and Omar Souleyman.

In the end, I would say that I am proud to have been a part of this, and that I definitely will follow future editions with close attention. I am thankful for how this has helped me move from what was troubling me at the time. Would love to thank everyone that involved which surely worked tirelessly to make this into the success it was. Thank you for the invite, which gave me hope during my darkest days and motivated me to keep creating music and finish more projects.
